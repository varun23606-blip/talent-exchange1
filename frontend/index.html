Simple Process

How Talent Exchange Works

1

Create your profile

Sign up with your student email and highlight your department and year.

2

Add skills you can teach

Share your strengths—whether Python, digital art, acoustic guitar, or calculus.

3

Add skills you want to learn

Tell the platform what new abilities or concepts you are eager to pick up.

4

Find matching people

Filter the student directory by skill, category, department, and semester.

5

Send an exchange request

Propose a reciprocal exchange: offer your skill in return for theirs.

6

Connect and start learning

Once accepted, message each other, jump on a video call, and start learning!
